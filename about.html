About me

I studied Mathematics, Chemistry and Biology at Lakeside College Luzira, and those three subjects shaped how I think more than I realised at the time. Maths trained me to break a problem into steps and not guess my way to an answer. Science practicals taught me to test an idea, observe what actually happens, and fix what does not work — which turns out to be exactly what writing and debugging code asks of you.

Making the move to Software Engineering at Bugema University felt like a natural next step once I saw the connection. The same logic I used to balance a chemistry equation or work through a proof is the logic I now use to design algorithms and build web applications. What changed is the medium, not the mindset.

Longer term, I am drawn to software that solves real problems in health and research — fields where my sciences background gives me context that a pure computing student might not have. I want to build things that are useful, reliable, and easy for people to trust.
